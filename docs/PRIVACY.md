# Privacy policy

_Last updated 27 September 2026_

This is the policy for the Score Reader Android app. It is the same text the app shows in
Settings › Privacy policy, and the page the Play Store listing links to.
(Source of truth for the in-app copy: `support.privacyScreen` in `src/i18n/locales/*.json`. Keep them in step.)

**The short version: your music and notes stay on your device. We don't collect anything about you.**

## No account

There's nothing to sign up for. The app doesn't know who you are.

## No analytics, no ads

No tracking, no advertising IDs, no usage statistics and no third-party code that reports back.

## No internet, except to buy Pro

The app has no internet access. The only exception is Google Play Billing when you buy or restore
Pro, which the Play Store app handles under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

## Everything stays on your device

Scores you import are copied into the app's private storage. Annotations, setlists, collections and
settings are stored there too. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Only the files you choose

The app can only read the files and folders you pick in Android's file picker, or share to it.

## Backups go where you save them

A backup is a single file saved wherever you choose: your device, a memory card or a cloud folder.
The app only writes it, and reads it back when you restore.

## Crash reports only if you send them

If the app crashes, a short technical report is kept on your device: app version, device model,
Android version, what the code was doing and a log of recent app activity. Next time, the app asks
if you'd like to send it. It only leaves your device if you share it yourself, by email or another
app you pick. Choosing "Not now" deletes it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the Play Store listing before it applies.
